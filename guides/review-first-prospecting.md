# Review-first website prospecting

Website Audit Leads helps agencies, consultants and freelancers build a small,
explainable queue of websites whose audit signals match a service they provide.

1. Choose one relevant opportunity family: SEO, speed, accessibility, security
   or redesign.
2. Preview the audience before exporting. Preview creates no paid lead rows.
3. Export a bounded batch of 50 websites.
4. Manually verify that the opportunity is current and your proposed help is
   relevant.
5. Personalize any outreach and send only approved rows to the CRM.
6. Save useful filters and rerun them as the available audience grows.

Audit scores are prioritization signals, not guarantees or certifications. Use
business contact data in accordance with applicable privacy, marketing and
anti-spam rules, respect opt-outs, and avoid indiscriminate bulk messaging.

The SDK calls the hosted Apify Actor and does not contain its internal
implementation, private data sources or infrastructure configuration.
